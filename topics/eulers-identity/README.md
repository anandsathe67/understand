What changed from v1 to v2 (all from the review and learner pass):

- Honest audience line, and a request to mark the places a parent has to carry the child.
- Powers explained first (2³ means 2 × 2 × 2), because the whole page is about one.
- Every letter (n, x, θ) arrives after the concrete number it stands for.
- Bracket expansion out of the main text and into a "For older readers" box.
- The climax written in words first ("run the machine with a turn of π and you walk halfway round"), symbols after.
- One sentence linking the turn machine to the bank: the bank keeps your money and adds a slice; this machine keeps your arrow and adds a sideways slice.
- Readouts rounded for a child; no notice bullet may contradict the readout at the sim's default setting.
- "Good place to stop" markers after i and after e.
